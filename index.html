Before connecting:
  1. Turn the LiPlay on and keep it awake.
  2. Close the instax app on your phone (or turn the phone's Bluetooth off). The camera takes only one connection.
  3. If the camera doesn't appear or won't connect, delete the registration in the camera's Bluetooth menu, and forget the INSTAX device in this computer's or phone's Bluetooth settings. Both sides must start fresh.
  4. In the picker, choose the entry ending in (IOS). That is the BLE endpoint the browser can reach.
  5. Once connected, put the camera back on the shooting screen (not the menu) before printing.
