Trouble connecting?

  1. Close the official instax app. The camera talks to one device at a time.
  2. On the camera, delete the pairing registration in its Bluetooth menu.
  3. On this device, forget INSTAX in Bluetooth settings.
  4. Turn the camera off and on, then pick the (IOS) entry when connecting.
  5. Keep the camera on the shooting screen while printing.
